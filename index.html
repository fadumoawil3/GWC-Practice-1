About GWC

Girls Who Code is a nonprofit organization on a mission to close the gender gap in technology. Through their programs, girls build coding skills and confidence in a supportive, inclusive environment.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to inspire, educate, and equip girls with the computing skills to pursue 21st-century opportunities. We envision a world where more women are creators, not just consumers, of technology.

Programs Offered

  • Clubs: Free after-school clubs for grades 3–12 to learn computer science.
  • Summer Immersion Program: 2-week coding intensives hosted by tech companies and universities.
  • College Loops: College-level communities of women studying CS and supporting one another.
